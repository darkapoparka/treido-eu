# Platform — Treido Global / general marketplace

A Bulgaria-first general physical-goods marketplace for personal sellers and businesses, designed for later country-by-country expansion.

## Purpose

A buyer can find a specific new or used item, understand its condition and seller, communicate or offer, and complete a supported transaction. The same person can sell from photos without first creating a business. Businesses get their own store identity, members, inbox and subscription. Producer food operations belong to a different platform. The initial unit is a unique listing, not an unlimited-stock product implied by the donor catalog.

## People

- Visitor/buyer: discover, inspect, save, ask, offer, purchase and get support.
- Personal seller: create recoverable photo listings and handle inquiries/orders.
- Business owner/member: operate the selected seller account within current permissions.
- Operator: separate content, support and financial responsibilities.

## Journeys

1. Photo draft -> category/condition/price -> publish -> another user finds listing -> conversation/offer -> accepted reservation -> supported purchase -> delivery/support.
2. Business owner invites a member -> member publishes within quota -> owner purchases Pro for this business -> another seller account remains unchanged.
3. Buyer saves a search and refines an AI-assisted query -> explicit budget/model constraints remain visible -> only real eligible listings are returned.

## Surfaces and scope

General-goods buyer marketplace, personal selling, business storefront/team tools, messaging/offers, subscriptions, item commerce and platform support. This is separate from Treido Foods.
