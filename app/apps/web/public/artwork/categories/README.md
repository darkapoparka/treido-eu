# Treido category illustrations

Original artwork generated on October 7, 2026 under the owner's explicit image-generation request. These are generic department illustrations, separate from seller/listing photos and editorial campaign media. They carry no availability, popularity, brand or inventory claim.

Three generated 1536×1024 transparent sheets were divided into equal cells and converted with the existing Sharp dependency to seventeen 320×320 WebP assets. Quality 80, alpha quality 90; total 384,272 bytes. The initial Electronics image was regenerated to remove its brand mark before inclusion. No captured Shop catalogue imagery is included.

Generation source/cell and output SHA-256 receipts are retained locally in `.qa/category-navigation-20261007/artwork-receipt.json`. Display uses the existing Explore tile, with decorative empty alt text and the department's accessible link label.

The October 7 optical-alignment pass measures each image's visible alpha bounds at 20/255. Within the existing 86px artwork frame, every department uses a shared 72% visible height, 90% bottom baseline and horizontal bounds center. Equal canvas dimensions alone do not establish that alignment. Calibration lives in `category-artwork.ts`; keep it in sync when an illustration changes.

Beauty received a minimal image-tool cleanup to remove a disconnected fragment at the bottom of its source-sheet crop. The other sixteen image files remain byte-identical. The current seventeen-image set totals 382,370 bytes, still at 320×320 with transparency. The cleanup source, current hashes, dimensions and calibration bounds are retained in `.qa/category-artwork-alignment-20261007/artwork-receipt.json`; the initial generation receipt above remains historical evidence.
