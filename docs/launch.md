## Measurement and economic decisions

Definitions: draft started/completed; valid listing published; eligible search/item visit; save/follow; qualified inquiry; first seller response; offer accepted; provider-confirmed order; seller-reported handover sale; refund/case; repeat buyer/seller. Record country/category/seller kind and source, with dedupe and privacy limits. GMV excludes subscriptions/boosts and seller-reported transactions; promotion spend is separate platform revenue.

Track time to first listing/inquiry/response/sale, search zero-result rate, successful publication/payment, cancellation/refund/fraud, repeat usage, support burden and actual contribution after processor/Connect/AI/media/email/support costs. Numeric growth promises follow observed cohorts. Proposed usability gate: 4 of 5 representative users find an item and start a valid draft unaided, with no unresolved critical publish/buy/recovery issue; record actual sessions rather than asserting this passed.

Billing prices and fees are concrete planning proposals in [billing](../billing.md). Validate fee economics for small and high-ticket orders, refunds, disputes and worst-case quota/AI usage before live activation. A published plan must explain price, included capabilities, renewal, cancellation and taxes for the actual jurisdiction.

## International rollout

Country profile owns country/locale/currency, category policy, seller onboarding/payment eligibility, delivery/pickup, tax/rights/disclosures, moderation language and operator/support coverage. A translated label alone does not launch a country. Keep country/currency explicit in data contracts now, start the next country with real supply and qualified end-to-end cases, then enable it through a recorded release task. Cross-border checkout has its own shipping/returns/customs/settlement contract.

## Release and recovery practice

T16 records the release candidate and complete evidence, narrows reference guards only on implemented routes, qualifies media/metadata/SEO and deployments, and performs post-release health plus real feature checks. Use reviewed feature configuration to contain a proven incident; report any resulting scope change. Preserve accepted order/provider state and reconciliation jobs through a rollback. Keep named operators for failed publication, payment/settlement, moderation and support. A backup is useful only with a tested restore and external-effect reconciliation.
